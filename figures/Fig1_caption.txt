Fig 1. Focused hybrid SEM of pesticide safety practices.

The diagram distinguishes ordinal latent psychological factors from observed context and practice indices. Both practice equations include all nine focal predictors and categorical country and A1–A11 controls. The psychological factors are also regressed on the six context scores and controls, with freely estimated residual covariances; the practice residuals covary. Auxiliary adjustment paths, measurement edges and residual terms are omitted for readability. The coefficient panels show fully standardized behavioral paths and nominal 95% delta-method intervals. Filled markers identify Holm-adjusted p < 0.05 across the 26 primary SEM and health coefficients. Arrow directions express modeling assumptions, not causal direction. WLSMV estimation uses 1,832 current chemical users with complete model information.
