Fig 2. Adjusted associations with pesticide symptoms heat dizziness and distress.

The panels display all eight focal health coefficients. Symptoms and distress use unstandardized mean-score differences with nominal 95% HC3 intervals; heat dizziness uses an odds ratio and interval on a logarithmic axis. Filled markers indicate Holm-adjusted p < 0.05 within the joint 26-coefficient family. Pesticide symptom and distress models use current users; the heat model includes all farmers. Each model adjusts for categorical country and A1–A11. Outcomes differ in units and recall periods, and the panels do not depict a causal sequence.
