Exploratory reanalysis specification fixed before these regressions are fitted.
The prior SEM has already been fitted repeatedly to the same dataset; these analyses are not preregistered or confirmatory.
Scores are transparent equally weighted observed summaries, NOT latent variables or validated clinical scales. Complete constituent items required; no mean imputation/prorating.
Training B1-B3 mean (0-2); support B4-B5; oversight B6-B7; risk awareness C1-C4; barriers C5-C8; benefits C9-C10; coping C11-C13; safety confidence C14-C16; descriptive norms C17,C19; PPE D1-D4; chemical handling D5-D8; heat protection D10,D12,D13; recent pesticide symptoms E1-E4 mean (0-2); distress E13-E15 mean (0-3). Do not include injunctive C18, ergonomic D9,D11, lifetime E5 or ever severe heat E12 in these composites.
Models: Training ~ support + oversight + controls (all farmers).
PPE and handling separately ~ support + oversight + training + risk + barriers + benefits + coping + confidence + norms + controls (current pesticide users A10=1/2).
Recent pesticide symptoms ~ PPE + handling + controls (current users).
Heat-related dizziness E10 ordered code treated as numeric recurrence index ~ heat protection + controls (all farmers). No diagnosis or same-period heat composite claim.
Distress ~ PPE + handling + coping + confidence + recent pesticide symptoms + controls (current users).
All controls are categorical factors: Country, A1-A11. No assumed equal intervals for education, farm size, nominal status or other control codes. Modelwise complete cases; main estimates OLS with HC3 heteroskedasticity-robust standard errors and t confidence intervals. Holm adjustment across 28 focal tests (control coefficients excluded from this exploratory family). Report every focal estimate, not significance-selected subsets.
Sensitivities: same models among all farmers where pesticide-user restrictions applied; joint complete cases across all scores/controls; exclude excess exact response profiles conservatively (not a correction/deduplication claim); country-stratified estimates; binary any recent pesticide symptom/heat dizziness logistic regressions with robust SE and explicit separation/convergence checks. These are descriptive associations, not causal paths/mediation/intervention tests. No geographic design correction can be asserted because actual community IDs and inclusion probabilities are absent.
